Telegram Control Center — Folder Sync Fix

This build fixes Telegram chat-folder synchronization.

Important:
- Telegram custom folders are DialogFilter objects. They are NOT the archive
  folder_id accepted by messages.getDialogs().
- Folder membership is evaluated from Telegram's actual DialogFilter rules:
  contacts, nonContacts, groups, broadcasts, bots, includePeers,
  pinnedPeers, excludePeers, excludeArchived and excludeRead.
- GramJS marked peer IDs are handled correctly:
  user -> ID, group -> -ID, channel -> -100ID.
- Existing .env/session/runtime data are intentionally excluded from this ZIP.
  Keep your local .env and session files from your working installation.
